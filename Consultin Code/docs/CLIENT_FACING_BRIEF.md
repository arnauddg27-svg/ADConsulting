# What You Get

**A.D. Homes & Consulting — Builder Data Platform**

A data platform and reporting system built around how you actually run construction. Client-owned infrastructure, built on BigQuery or Snowflake, delivered in 4–8 weeks.

---

## The outcome

Your ERP, spreadsheets, finance systems, APIs, and exports feed one centralized warehouse. Your team opens one dashboard that shows:

- **Every job**, every stage, every milestone — start to close
- **Where you are on budget and where you're not** — by job, community, and plan
- **Loan exposure and draw status** — nothing expires without warning
- **Sales pipeline and closings** — pace, agents, communities, entities
- **Permit and land pipeline** — what's stuck, what's moving
- **Per-job P&L audits** — the real net margin on every home you close

Same dashboard works for ops leads, finance, and the exec team. Different questions, one source of truth.

## How we work

1. **Discovery call** — 30 minutes. We learn your systems and what breaks.
2. **Warehouse audit** — we profile every source table: what's there, what's usable, what's blocked.
3. **KPI assessment** — we score every metric you want as ready / partial / blocked. You see exactly what ships and what needs source data work.
4. **Build** — 4–6 weeks. Warehouse, KPI logic, dashboards, drill-downs, alerts.
5. **Review & tune** — three sessions (ops / finance / exec), calibrate thresholds.
6. **Cutover** — production URL, SSO for your team, walkthroughs recorded.
